A note on pitches

What tends to be a good fit

The Quiet Stack is a personal blog, not a content farm. The strongest pitches are specific, reflective, and connected to the themes readers come here for: calmer technology, sustainable productivity, digital boundaries, and the everyday design of attention.

I am especially interested in stories about tools that changed how you work, routines that survived contact with real life, and thoughtful disagreements with essays already published here.

Rowan Vale

Writer of The Quiet Stack

Rowan writes long-form essays for readers who want technology to feel useful without becoming the weather of the whole day.
